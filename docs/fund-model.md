# Million Dollar Fist Fund Model

## Overview

Every dollar raised goes to the community. Overhead is minimized. Projects are funded by percentage of total funds raised, ensuring the model scales from small communities to large ones.

## Fund Allocation

| Project | % | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Food Project | 50% | Every resident knows where to get food |
| Education Project | 10% | Every resident has learning resources |
| Housing Project | 10% | Every individual has safe shelter |
| Hygiene Project | 10% | Every resident has hygiene resources |
| Health Project | 5% | Proactive and reactive health resources |
| Business Development | 3% | Resources for community-aligned businesses |
| Emergency Relief Fund | 5% | Money set aside for unforeseen situations |
| Investment Project | 5% | Grow community wealth, reinvest returns |
| Residential Payout | 1% | Direct gift to households each disbursement period |
| Business Payout | 1% | Direct gift to businesses each disbursement period |

## Distribution Rules

### Residential Payout
Each household is eligible for a financial gift each disbursement period. A household may decline — if so, the gift rolls to another household. If no household accepts, the funds go to the Emergency Relief Fund.

### Business Payout
Each business is eligible for a financial gift each disbursement period. A business may decline — if so, the gift rolls to another business. If no business accepts, the funds roll to the Residential Payout.

## Scaling

The 1% payout model is designed to scale. At $10,000 annual revenue per contributing entity, the weekly payout is ~$1.92. At $1,000,000 annual revenue per entity, the weekly payout is ~$192. The model is viable at any scale.
