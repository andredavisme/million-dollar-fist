The Repeating Pattern

  1. A community exists.
  2. A government is established.
  3. The community has needs.
  4. The government responds — within its own bureaucratic constraints.
  5. The community is the last to add to the solution.

Million Dollar Fist breaks the pattern.

A Framework, Not a Manual

MDF shows what’s possible. Your community decides how to get there.

Million Dollar Fist is not a global how-to guide. It defines a model of community self-management — what it looks like when a community takes responsibility for food, housing, health, education, and economic growth on its own terms.

How that management is built, organized, and sustained is for each community to answer. Communities live differently, and they will thrive through the management they create together.

“This blueprint is a starting point, not a script. Every community is different — these tools help you begin the conversation. What you build from there belongs to your community.”

About the Numbers

Every projected figure has a source. Here’s how we got them.

The participation rates, budget deltas, jobs created, and dollars circulated shown across this site are scenario projections — not verified outcomes. They are derived from a model built on 10 adjustable levers grounded in economic research and comparable community programs.

Anytime you see a projected number, it comes from that model. The methodology page explains each lever, its default value, and the research basis behind it — so you can evaluate the assumptions yourself.
